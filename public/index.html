Recursion

 

Time is applied as three nested rotations: the hour about Z, the minute about Y, the second about X. Every arm is the axle its two children turn about, so each joint is a right angle, and each generation repeats hour, minute, second at a smaller scale. At twelve o'clock every twist is zero and the tree is a perfect lattice: a dense core at midnight, opening until it fills space at noon. Tap a joint to travel to it.

QUADRANT

A symbolic calendar, not a lunar one. Each sigil places a body on a twelve-fold orbit (30° per month), sets a symbolic phase that runs new → full → new over the year, and inscribes a seasonal triangle; equinox and solstice months carry an axis. Quadrants follow the boreal astronomical seasons. All twelve stand around the year ring: pull back, or choose Year, to find them.

Hours; minutes, seconds, thirds — each place is one sixtieth of the last. The wedges write the same hour, minute and second as Babylonian numerals. DIES is the elapsed day as a sexagesimal fraction: its first place turns every 24 minutes.
