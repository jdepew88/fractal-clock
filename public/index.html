Recursion

 

Every hand ends in a smaller clock, turned to face along that hand. The heaviest branches turn by the hour angle, the mid-tone by the minute, the palest by the second. A pulse leaves the centre each second; its depth is the fraction of the second elapsed.

QUADRANT

A symbolic calendar, not a lunar one. Each sigil places a body on a twelve-fold orbit (30° per month), sets a symbolic phase that runs new → full → new over the year, and inscribes a seasonal triangle; equinox and solstice months carry an axis. Quadrants follow the boreal astronomical seasons.

HR 0.00°

A full cycle is 360°. Seconds and minutes advance 6° per unit; the hour hand advances 30° per hour, or 0.5° per minute. Measured clockwise from twelve.

Binary

Rows are hour (24-hour), minute and second. Columns weigh 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1; a lit mark is a one.

DIES 0;00,00,00

Hours; minutes, seconds, thirds — each place is one sixtieth of the last. The wedges write the same hour, minute and second as Babylonian numerals. DIES is the elapsed day as a sexagesimal fraction: its first place turns every 24 minutes.

Shadow

0.00°

 

An abstract sundial. The gnomon's shadow sweeps the outer 24-hour ring once a day, 15° per hour, longest at 06:00 and 18:00 and shortest at noon. It is geometry, not a solar ephemeris.
